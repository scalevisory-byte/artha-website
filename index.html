The recovery challenge

Is your money stuck with customers?

Outstanding receivables quietly drain the cash a healthy business needs. If any of these sound familiar, it may be time for a structured approach.

  • Invoices overdue for months
  • Customers repeatedly promising payment
  • Large outstanding balances
  • Unresponsive customers
  • Payment disputes
  • Old receivables accumulating
  • Weak internal follow-up
  • A case that needs legal escalation

Cash flow

Outstanding receivables can restrict growth.

Revenue on paper is not the same as money in the bank. When payments stall, the pressure moves straight to your operations, payroll and growth plans.

Revenue is only useful when it becomes cash.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What type of receivables does ARTHA handle?

ARTHA focuses on commercial, business-to-business receivables — overdue invoices, outstanding balances and disputed commercial payments across a range of industries.

How does the recovery process work?

We follow a structured framework: understanding your case, reviewing documentation, professional follow-up and negotiation, and strategic legal escalation where required — with clear communication throughout.

When should a business approach ARTHA?

As soon as a payment is materially overdue or a customer becomes unresponsive. Earlier engagement generally improves the likelihood and speed of recovery.

Can ARTHA handle long-overdue invoices?

Yes. We regularly work on ageing receivables. While older cases can be more complex, a structured approach and appropriate escalation can still make a meaningful difference.

Does ARTHA provide legal escalation?

Where a case warrants it, we coordinate legal notices and strategic escalation as part of a considered recovery strategy — applied at the right stage, not as a first resort.

Is the recovery process confidential?

Yes. Your accounts, customer details and case information are handled with strict confidentiality and professional discretion at every stage.

How do I submit a recovery case?

Use the case submission form below, or reach us directly on WhatsApp or by phone. We begin with a confidential assessment and recommend the appropriate recovery approach.
